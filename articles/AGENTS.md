# Daily TeamStation AI articles

Read this file before creating or updating any article in this directory. These are the owner's October 10, 2026 instructions for the daily GitHub Pages distribution, including its branding and voice. They take precedence over older conflicting editorial instructions elsewhere in this repository.

## Voice and purpose

Write for CTOs and CIOs as a helpful colleague chatting by the water cooler: informal, cordial, technically useful, and easy to read. Use contractions, natural US slang, and familiar abbreviations such as PR, CI, SDK and CTO when they fit. Explain unfamiliar terms. Keep humor light and kind. Avoid forced slang, profanity, aggressive sales language, invented anecdotes, filler and em/en dashes. Articles and distribution posts share this voice; adapt their lengths to each platform.

Teach a concrete decision about the agentic AI SDLC, relevant tools, methods, engineering roles or skills. Research the day's current evidence from primary sources. Show the actual source publication dates. A methods review must not masquerade as same-day news. Preserve bounded research findings and distinguish proposed operating advice from sourced claims.

## Brand and page format

- Use the exact official horizontal lockup from `TeamStation-AI/public/brand/assets/Logo/lockup-horizontal-dark.svg` for the article header. The current template embeds that SVG unchanged so its text inherits Geist. Do not reconstruct the wordmark from a standalone mark and HTML text, or recolor, stretch, crop, or decorate the logo. The standalone mark remains appropriate for the favicon.
- Use the shared `assets/article.css` and the current TeamStation brand library: background `#0d1117`, panel `#11161d`, elevated `#151b23`, text `#e6edf3`, secondary `#9aa4b2`, border `#232b36`, accent `#6e8aff`, hover `#9bb8ff`. Preserve the distinct colors inside the official logo. Use Geist for body/display and Geist Mono for code and technical labels, discrete font-size breakpoints, 28px page gutters, 8px card radii and 32px card padding. Verify the rendered lockup, palette, font loading and text bounds at desktop and phone widths.
- Base new article HTML on `2026-10-10-agentic-ai-sdlc-harness/index.html`. Keep the header, author/date, helpful answer-first intro, short takeaway box, contents links, source date note, useful section headings, contextual links and service/research footer.
- Include accurate title, description, canonical, Open Graph and Twitter metadata, and Article JSON-LD. Match the headline, author, dates and image to the actual page. Do not copy October 10's dates, topic or image into another day's article.
- Create a relevant branded social preview for each article. Preserve the real logo. The current example is a code-generated title card, not a claim of ranking or indexing.
- Link naturally through descriptive industry and buyer terms to verified live TeamStation service pages, relevant roles, research and related articles. No keyword stuffing or fixed density target.

## Publish and verify

Edit an existing article in place without changing its URL unnecessarily. Verify sources and TeamStation targets, HTML metadata/schema, assets and internal section links. Inspect desktop and mobile rendering when available. Publish through the repository's existing GitHub Pages source and verify the served article and asset bytes; a commit alone is not proof of publication.

The daily loop starts at 08:00 America/Mexico_City, executes one publishing objective at a time, and restores repository instructions and durable publication history in every fresh worker. This document defines the article format; it does not install a scheduler, prove delivery to other platforms, or replace the private publication journal. Record the exact deployed revision and verified URL in the real journal before advancing.
